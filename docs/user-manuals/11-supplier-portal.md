# Supplier portal

For suppliers' staff who have been given a portal account by the hospital. You see **only
your own company's** quotations, tenders, orders and invoices.

## 1. Signing in

Open the address the hospital gave you and sign in with your username and password. Change
the initial password when asked, and turn on two-factor authentication under *My account*.
After 5 wrong passwords the account locks for 15 minutes.

## 2. Supplier home

A summary of open requests for quotation, tenders, orders to deliver and invoices. *Our
details with the hospital* shows what the hospital holds about you (KRA PIN, prequalification
and its expiry, compliance documents, the bank account masked). Ask the procurement office
to update anything that is wrong. Expired prequalification means you can't be invited or
ordered from.

## 3. Requests for quotation (*Requests for quotation*)

1. Open an RFQ you were invited to. Check the items or services, quantities, specification,
   delivery address and the **closing date**.
2. Enter your unit price for each line, *Delivery (days)*, *Payment terms*, *Valid until*,
   and any notes. Attach your quotation document.
3. *Send* before the closing date. You can't change it after bidding closes.

The hospital compares quotations on price, compliance and delivery (and may score
quality). You'll receive an order if you are awarded.

## 4. Tenders (*Tenders*)

1. Open tenders, and those you are invited to, are listed. *Request bid documents* to
   download the tender documents.
2. *Ask a question* during the clarification period. *Clarifications and addenda* are sent to
   everyone holding the documents. Read them.
3. *Submit a bid*:
   * prices per *Tender lines*;
   * *Technical proposal* and compliance documents;
   * *Bid security* amount and reference;
   * *Bid validity*.

   *Submit bid* before the closing time. Late bids are refused.
4. Bids are **sealed**: nobody at the hospital sees prices before the formal opening.
   *Withdraw* is possible until the closing time.
5. You'll get an award or regret letter by email.

## 5. Purchase orders (*Purchase orders*)

1. New orders appear here (and arrive by email as a PDF).
2. *Acknowledge order*, with your reference and the expected delivery date (or *Reject*, with
   a reason).
3. *Update delivery status*: processing → dispatched → in transit.
4. When you deliver, *Send delivery note or invoice*: number / reference, date, amount, and
   attach the scanned delivery note or invoice.

Deliver what the order says. Over-deliveries are refused at the store, and goods
rejected at inspection are returned with a return note.

## 6. Invoices and payments (*Invoices & payments*)

Your invoices with their status:
* **Captured / Pending approval**: being checked against the order and the delivery;
* **Approved**: due for payment on the due date;
* **Scheduled**: on a payment voucher;
* **Partially paid / Paid**: with the payment date and reference.

Payment is by bank transfer, cheque or M-PESA (PayBill / Till). **Withholding tax** on
services (5 %) is deducted and declared to KRA; the payment shows the amount settled, the WHT
and the net paid.

Invoices are matched to what the hospital **ordered and received**. If quantities or prices
differ from the order, payment waits until the difference is resolved. Make sure your sale
is declared on **KRA eTIMS**: invoices not found on eTIMS are flagged.

## 7. Help

Contact the procurement office for orders and RFQs, and the finance office for payments.
